# Experimental posture with original50Nm waist limits

This experiment DOES NOT replace the accepted default standby, shared low home,12 lift baselines or training configs. The preceding low home needs about225Nm static at waist joint2 even empty, according to this URDF mass/COM model. Original50Nm effort values come from AgibotTech/genie_sim_robot_model commit6d5b2159806ae0e64b432b638e9ee288d8bddc8f robots/genie/g2/xacro/G2.body.urdf.xacro. Hardware rated/peak limits have not been confirmed.

We searched the first three waist and both mirrored arms under original joint bounds, waist torque constraints and torso tilt<=20deg, keeping other waist/head/base joints fixed. Optimistic empty quasi-static searches, without collision or motion constraints, found shoulders down to about1.262m, end-link down to0.567m and selected contact point down to0.412m at50Nm. A40Nm static budget yielded optimistic contact0.459m. These are numerical search results under the stated parameterization, not proof of global infeasibility for every robot configuration. No floor-object grasp is validated by those optimistic bounds.

A collision-screened side-support work candidate at40cm forward offset and +/-12cm contact Y has contact height0.86054m. Waist angles about[-2.286,+2.286,+9.190]deg; torso tilt9.19deg. Estimated gravity torques with1kg total payload split equally across the contacts are about[-30.944,-40,-40]Nm in the first three waist axes. File fixtures/effortpose_proposal.json contains the full joint vector; it is explicitly experimental and not training-ready.

The actual20s preparation and empty holding simulation used original50Nm waist actuator caps and retained original waist angular ranges, with NO artificial waist joint locks. Gravity feedforward plus feedback are budgeted within those caps; no mass/gravity/collision edits. The base remains pinned. The nearby work target stays away from waist stops; empty transition/hold passed independent tracking, original joint bounds, self-contact and gravity checks. A1kg load was included only in static estimation, NOT in the physics holding test; no grasp was attempted. See trial_index.json for measured results and sample/video SHA256. Therefore this candidate is suitable for exploring raised-object manipulation, not a replacement for the existing ground-lift dataset.
