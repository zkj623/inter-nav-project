## Source audit

The pinned official G2 body xacro declares effort50 for all five body joints. The official Genie Sim runtime does not necessarily use that cap: commit795b3fc77e4dd9f3ef972302ab90420c35450f62 has fallback art_body.max_effort1200Nm and its supplied physics_params.yaml body block uses1e7Nm. These are simulation control settings, NOT confirmed rated/peak physical actuator limits. The public manufacturer manual documents2 folding/lifting leg DOF plus3 waist DOF and0~2.3m workspace with O10, but no leg/waist output-torque rating was found. Joint roles1/2 are the lower lifting chain (ankle/knee-like); joints3/4/5 are upper waist pitch/roll/yaw by chain/range correspondence. Calling all five simply waist was imprecise.

At the unchanged low work home, current-URDF empty gravity torques are about81.58,225.24,35.42,0.38,0.10Nm in absolute magnitude. The225Nm value belongs to the second LOWER-LIFTING-chain joint, not the upper waist-pitch joint. It is explained by about58.41kg downstream mass at39.31cm horizontal lever. Native USD link mass/COM match the URDF; potential-gradient gravity independently matches the analytic calculation. Native robot mass is about165.29kg vs the public product specification185kg; this is not a physically identified mass model. Native root-object mass is1kg, gravity is9.81m/s2, no robot link is authored gravity-disabled or kinematic. Original waist angular ranges are retained. No inspected model contains a calibrated passive-compensation or transmission model; this does not establish absence on hardware.

## Finite-reference experiment

The original accepted startup/low-home and round-lidded-bin plan were rerun with a finite1200Nm waist cap (official fallback value), free waist ranges and no artificial waist locks. This complete workflow passed original four-contact/lift/tilt/drift/visible-surface/nonhand checks. Actual-pose empty-robot gravity estimates peak about245.7Nm during manipulation. This confirms a finite-cap simulation result, NOT hardware feasibility or strict50 success.

## Strict50 alternatives

Search broadened the previous20deg/symmetric posture parameterization to all five body joints, head and independent arms. It explored front/side/rear contacts, single-arm counterweight and dual-hand work. Optimistic single-hand reach down to18.3cm under50Nm used nearly all capacity and about46deg tilt; it was only reach, not a grasp proof. Feasible static states alone do not establish motion/interaction success. See per-run fixtures rather than assuming a global-infeasibility result.

Low wood-stool experiments keep the object on the floor,1kg mass assumption, original materials/geometry/arm and gripper caps. One gripper pinches seat thickness while the other arm counterbalances, with ZERO inactive-hand object support and no non-wheel environment or self support. This is an explicit TWO-active-contact single-gripper task, not a weakened four-contact claim for bimanual grasp. Original >=15cm lift,>=3s hold,<=20deg object tilt,<=2cm drift,>=90% positive-contact samples,1.5mm loaded visual gap,2.5mm contact witness,1mm nonhand penetration and0.001Ns nonhand/self/environment impulse gates remain. Torso targets are held by free-range actuators capped50Nm; no waist limit welding. Root remains pinned. The experimental ready pose is seeded once before physics: standing-to-ready transition is not validated. These poses exceed the preceding20deg torso envelope and may approach joint bounds, so they are not automatically suitable as the unified training start.

Initial controllers/poses failed on nonhand/self contact, joint tracking or contact-surface witnesses; none is counted as successful. Planned collision-safe redundancy was replayed directly instead of being silently re-solved into a colliding null-space branch. The conservative gravity split also had a control issue: g+clip(PD,+/-(E-|g|)) permits only[38,50]Nm when g=44,E=50, even though the specified symmetric cap permits smaller or reverse total output. The combined controller encodes g as an implicit-drive position-reference bias g/Kp and uses a SINGLE native+/-50Nm clamp, with ZERO added manual waist effort. Every saved sample records the native clamp and zero manual waist feedforward. This restores bidirectional correction WITHOUT exceeding50. Other joint caps, gravity and collision geometry are unchanged. Gripper gain40/2 and128/32 solver iterations are recorded; a retained legacy text label64/16 is superseded by those effective settings. The track-controller variant increases arm PD gains to5000/400 while retaining the original arm effort caps. Legacy IDEAL video banners and ideal-body text in earlier files are template labels, not the effective50Nm control cap; inspect the hashed source and per-sample caps. All original-mesh self intersections are independently rejected, including ones absent from cooked contact reports. Some firm-controller holds last6s (>=3s acceptance) due their time scaling; use actual sample times, not legacy labels.
